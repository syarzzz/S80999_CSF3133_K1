About the Festival

Welcome to the Taste of Terengganu! This festival celebrates the delicious traditional food and cultural heritage of Terengganu. Visitors can enjoy a varienty of local dishes prepare by talented local food vendors.

From the famous Nasi Dagang to traditional Kerepok Lekor, this event offers an opportunity to experience the authentic flavours of Terengganu. Bring your family and friends to enjoy delicious food and a wonderful festival atmosphere.

Free entry! Everyone is welcome to join the festival and enjoy the local food.
