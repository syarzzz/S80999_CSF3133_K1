About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. Originally established as a centre for fisheries and marine science education, UMT has grown into a premier comprehensive university focusing on marine science, aquatic resources, and ocean informatics.

The university is dedicated to advancing knowledge and nurturing talents through cutting-edge research, academic excellence, and sustainable community engagement, aligning closely with its vision of becoming a focal point for marine science research and education in the region.
